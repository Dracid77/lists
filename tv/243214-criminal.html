The story

Follow the story of two friends, Leo Patterson and Ricky Lawless, one trying to fight his way out of the world of crime they were raised in, the other desperate to follow in the footsteps of his father, a legendary outlaw. And when Ricky's older brother Tracy returns home from the Army, their tragic family history comes home with him. Drawn into their orbit are Greta, a sharp-tongued car thief from Leo's past, and Mallory, Ricky's girlfriend, who fears what he'll do to get by. Gunfights, heists, and tragic romance are the order of the day, but will anyone make it out alive?
