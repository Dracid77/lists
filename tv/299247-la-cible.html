The story

Hugo, a professional bodyguard, is going through a painful divorce. Still in love with his ex-wife, he tries to maintain a bond with his children. But everything changes the day he attends his son’s birthday party: his ex-wife and their two children are kidnapped right before his eyes. The police investigation quickly turns its attention to Hugo, who is regarded as the prime suspect. Overwhelmed by the evidence and facing the prospect of imprisonment, he decides to escape to find his family. Whilst on the run, he conducts his own investigation and uncovers disturbing truths about the woman he thought he knew. This psychological and family thriller pits Hugo against a race against time where every second counts.
