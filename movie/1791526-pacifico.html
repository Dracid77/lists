The story

Pacífico follows a group of divers who travel to Malpelo Island to embark on the adventure of a lifetime. During their final dive, they disappear into the Pacific Ocean when a powerful current pulls them apart and carries them in different directions. With no boat, no sense of direction, and hundreds of miles from land, each must face an unforgiving ocean. Meanwhile, those searching for them can only try to guess where they might be.
